What happens when a drain is about to overflow

Everyone can follow these stages on the live map.

  1. Alert raisedThe sensor sees the water close to the top.
  2. Crew assignedMunicipal staff send a cleaning crew.
  3. Work in progressThe crew is clearing the drain.
  4. CleanedThe drain is clear and back to normal.
